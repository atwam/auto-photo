For a photo that passes

  • Neutral face, mouth closed, no smile
  • Look straight at the camera, head level
  • Eyes visible: open, no glare, hair off the face
  • Plain, light background, no shadows behind you
  • Even light on the face, no hat or headwear (religious exceptions apply)
  • Camera at eye level, about 1–1.5 m away; someone else should take it
